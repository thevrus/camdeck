Camdeck sets up your webcam and gets out of the way.

No virtual camera, no video pipeline, no effects engine. Settings are written to the camera, so every app sees them.

What it does

  • Live previewFlip it like a mirror and pick the video quality.
  • Picture controlsBrightness, contrast, color, sharpness, focus and flicker.
  • ExposureLet the camera decide, or set it yourself.
  • Zoom and move the viewOne draggable pad.
  • Auto framingFollows your face and keeps you centered.
  • PresetsStore your settings and bring them back in one click.
  • Per-app presetsApply a preset when a chosen app opens the camera.
  • Zebra stripesSpot blown-out highlights in the preview.
  • Mac video effectsPortrait, Studio Light and Background, from Apple.
  • Menu barRun from the menu bar and open at login, if you like. Nothing runs unless you want it to.

What it will not do: virtual camera, backgrounds, filters, overlays, recording. Anything that renders or re-encodes video is out, on purpose. It is why Camdeck stays small and the picture stays untouched.

Supported cameras

Almost any USB webcam works for the basics: picture, exposure, zoom, focus and color. That includes Logitech Brio and C920, Elgato Facecam, Razer Kiyo and Anker PowerConf. Some get extras.

Insta360 Link 2CFully tested. Adds digital pan and tilt, HDR and shutter speed.
Insta360 Link 2, LinkShould work, not tested yet.
AnkerWork C310, PowerConf C200Early support, not tested on real hardware.
OBSBOT Tiny 2, Tiny SE, MeetPlanned.
Opal Tadpole, C1Planned.

Own a camera that is not listed? Open an issue and we will look at adding it.

Automation

Camdeck answers to camdeck:// links, so Shortcuts, Raycast and the Terminal can control it without opening the window. Shortcuts also has native actions: Apply Preset, Reset View and Reset Image.

  • camdeck://preset/MeetingApply a preset by name, or by number: preset/2
  • camdeck://reset-viewCenter the view and zoom out
  • camdeck://reset-imageBrightness, color and exposure back to defaults
  • camdeck://resetReset all settings. Presets are kept
  • camdeck://lock/onTurn "Keep These Settings" on. lock/off turns it off
  • camdeck://camera/NameSwitch to a camera by name or ID

Add ?camera=Name to any link to pick the camera first.

open -g "camdeck://preset/Meeting"

Open source. Free.

  • MIT licensed. Use it, share it, read the code.
  • No driver. Nothing is added to your system.
  • Updates itself. New versions install quietly, or choose Camdeck, Check for Updates.
  • Replaces the maker's app. Insta360, OBSBOT and Opal ship their own heavy control apps, each for one brand. You can uninstall them.

First launch: if macOS says it could not verify Camdeck, open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. You do this once.
