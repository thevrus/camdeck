Download for Mac

  1. Drag it to Applications

    Open the download and drag Camdeck into your Applications folder.

  2. Say yes to macOS, once

    If macOS can’t verify Camdeck, open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

  3. Pick your camera

    Allow camera access, choose your webcam, and you’re set.

Questions

How do I change my webcam’s brightness on a Mac?
macOS has no brightness, contrast or focus controls for USB webcams. Camdeck adds them: pick your camera, move a slider, and the camera keeps the new setting for every app.
Do I need to pick a new camera in Zoom?
No. Camdeck adjusts the camera you already use, so Zoom and every other app keep using it as before. There’s nothing to switch and no added delay.
Will my settings stick after a restart?
Most cameras remember their settings on their own. If yours forgets, turn on Keep These Settings and Camdeck puts them back whenever an app opens the camera.
Is Camdeck free?
Yes. Camdeck is free and open source. There’s no account, no ads and nothing to buy.
Which Macs does Camdeck run on?
Any Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 26 Tahoe. Intel Macs aren’t supported.
Does Camdeck collect anything?
No. There’s no account and no tracking. Camdeck only goes online to check for updates.
